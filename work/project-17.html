Shifting a household habit from reactive to proactive

A sponsored studio project with Procter & Gamble, reframing a household air-care product around when people actually notice their home — moving use from reactive (something already smells) to proactive.

My part is the research end: usage-journey mapping to find the behavioural triggers and barriers, and translating what we find into concept prototypes the team can put in front of people.

  • Behavioural triggers & barriers
  • Concept prototyping
