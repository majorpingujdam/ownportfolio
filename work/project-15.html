Overview

Project Cataclysm: Netsphere Monitoring System is a cyberpunk interactive-fiction game staged as a retro terminal interface. You play as KAEL, navigating authentication sequences, monitoring feeds, and branching decision points inside a restricted digital network — until the story forces a choice: cooperate with OVERSEER, resist it, or corrupt it from within.
